## Overview

Apiary monitoring for hobbyist beekeepers: yards, hives, inspections and harvests, with an expected-yield estimate and an assistant that summarises field notes.

The project is in progress. Foundations, data and the database schema are done; the prediction model and the interface are being built. The full case study will arrive when there is something real to show.
